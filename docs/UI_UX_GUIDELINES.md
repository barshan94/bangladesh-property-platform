# UI/UX Guidelines

## Visual direction
- Professional, calm, credible corporate identity.
- Proposed palette: deep navy/slate, white, restrained gold/amber accent.
- Avoid visual clutter, aggressive sales patterns, and unsupported trust badges.
- Typography and spacing must prioritize readability on mobile.

## Information architecture
Primary navigation candidates: Home, Properties, Services, How It Works, Knowledge Hub, About, Contact. Keep Call/Contact Experts visible without overwhelming navigation.

## Homepage sequence
1. Header and clear value proposition.
2. Company introduction and primary calls to action.
3. Property search / buy / sell entry points.
4. Trust evidence and how the service works.
5. Property legal and assistance services.
6. Featured properties.
7. Expert consultation CTA.
8. Educational videos and latest articles.
9. Testimonials and FAQs.
10. Confidential evaluation CTA.
11. Contact details and footer.

## Interaction principles
- Mobile-first responsive layouts.
- Accessible labels, focus states, keyboard navigation, contrast, and reduced-motion support.
- Forms explain why information is requested and how it will be used.
- Upload controls show file types, size limits, progress, and failure recovery.
- Clearly distinguish public inquiry from confidential document submission.
- Sticky mobile call/inquiry actions must not obscure content or controls.
- Show clear status labels and next steps; do not imply legal progress that has not occurred.

## Content integrity
Do not invent staff credentials, years of experience, testimonials, office locations, property inventory, verification claims, or service availability.
