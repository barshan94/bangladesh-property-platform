# Bangladesh Legal and Regulatory Review Register

## Purpose
Track legal questions that must be answered before the platform markets or performs property-related services. This document is a review checklist, not legal advice.

## Mandatory counsel review
- Property ownership, transfer, registration, land records, mutation, and applicable local procedures.
- Eligibility and restrictions for Bangladeshi citizens, NRBs, dual nationals, foreign nationals, foreign investors, and inherited property.
- Power of attorney execution, attestation, authentication, registration, scope, and revocation.
- Succession/inheritance and partition procedures for relevant personal-law contexts.
- Developer, housing, consumer, and construction disputes and the correct Bangladesh authorities/forums.
- Tenancy, eviction, rent, and dispute procedures.
- Property taxes, capital gains, stamp/registration costs, and applicable tax advice boundaries.
- Brokerage/agency, advertising, consumer protection, and listing permissions.
- Legal professional engagement, referral arrangements, confidentiality, conflicts, and fee disclosures.
- Electronic records, digital signatures, cybersecurity, privacy, cross-border data access, and document retention.
- AML/CFT, sanctions, and source-of-funds obligations where applicable.

## Jurisdiction warning
Do not copy legal service labels or regulatory references from an Indian/NRI-focused reference site into Bangladesh marketing without checking local applicability. RERA is not a Bangladesh regulator.

## Required evidence before launch
- Written review by qualified Bangladesh counsel.
- Approved public service descriptions and disclaimers.
- Approved client engagement and consent documents.
- Defined escalation for foreign-client eligibility questions.
- Documented legal owner for periodic updates.
