# Decision Log

Record material product, legal, security and technical decisions here. Use newest entries first. A proposal is not an approved decision.

## 2026-11-12 — Repository visibility

- **Decision:** Keep the GitHub repository public for now, at the owner's explicit request.
- **Implication:** Never commit credentials, real client information, confidential property documents, private contact exports or production data. Treat all repository content as publicly readable.
- **Status:** Approved.

## 2026-11-12 — Product direction

- **Decision:** Build a Bangladesh-focused real estate marketplace combined with property legal/verification and remote property assistance.
- **Implication:** Corporate content, expert contact, articles, videos, FAQs, testimonials and consultation flows are core product features, not optional decoration.
- **Status:** Approved as product direction; detailed MVP scope remains subject to validation.

## 2026-11-12 — Target audience

- **Decision:** Serve local users and overseas clients with property interests in Bangladesh.
- **Implication:** Plan for remote communication, international contact details, document intake, time-zone-aware consultation and transparent local execution.
- **Legal constraint:** Foreign-client eligibility and acquisition rights must be assessed case by case by qualified Bangladesh counsel. Do not imply foreign nationality alone grants a right to acquire property.
- **Status:** Approved as target audience; eligibility policy unresolved.

## 2026-11-12 — Trust and verification language

- **Decision:** Listing moderation and legal title verification are separate concepts.
- **Implication:** A published listing cannot automatically receive a Verified Title badge. Any verification claim must state scope, source records, reviewer, date and limitations.
- **Status:** Approved.

## 2026-11-12 — Initial implementation scope

- **Proposal:** Prioritize corporate website, service catalogue, knowledge/video/FAQ content, contact and consultation intake, then moderated property listings.
- **Deferred:** Payments, escrow, rent collection, advanced property management and automated legal conclusions.
- **Status:** Proposed; final MVP approval pending.
