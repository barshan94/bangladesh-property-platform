# Property Legal and Advisory Services Catalogue

This catalogue is a proposed information architecture, not confirmation that any service is currently offered. Every description, eligibility rule, fee, and workflow must be reviewed by qualified Bangladesh counsel and the operating company.

## Ownership and verification
- Title search and record review.
- Ownership and chain-of-title assistance.
- Land survey, boundary, and possession coordination.

## Buying, selling, and transfer
- Property purchase/sale legal coordination.
- Deed preparation/registration coordination.
- Transfer and mutation assistance.

## Inheritance and ownership rights
- Succession and inheritance documentation assistance.
- Partition and co-owner dispute assistance.
- Adverse possession issue evaluation.

## Disputes and litigation
- Developer-related claims and project disputes.
- Property litigation and dispute coordination.
- Landlord-tenant disputes.

## Finance and regulatory matters
- Property mortgage/banking documentation assistance.
- Property-specific tax and transaction advisory through qualified professionals.
- Building approval and regulatory compliance review.

## Important boundaries
- Do not list unrelated criminal, family, immigration, or general corporate legal services as part of this product.
- Do not advertise RERA as a Bangladesh regulator; it is associated with India. Use Bangladesh-applicable law and forums only after counsel confirms the correct framework.
- Do not imply that administrative assistance is a substitute for legal representation.
- Do not promise outcomes, ownership rights, or title clearance.
- Clearly identify the professional responsible for legal advice and whether the service is provided by the company, a law firm, or a referral partner.
