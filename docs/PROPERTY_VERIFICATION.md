# Property Verification and Listing Trust Policy

## Separate concepts
1. **Listing moderation:** checks whether submitted content meets publication rules.
2. **Document review:** records which documents were received and inspected.
3. **Record search:** records searches performed against specified offices/databases and dates.
4. **Physical inspection:** reports observed condition, access, boundaries, occupancy, and limitations.
5. **Legal title opinion:** a professional legal conclusion issued only by an appropriately qualified and engaged professional.

These are not interchangeable. A listing approved for publication is not thereby legally verified.

## Required verification record
For every verification claim, record:
- Property identifier and location.
- Client/seller authority and consent.
- Documents and sources reviewed.
- Search office/database and search date, where applicable.
- Reviewer identity, role, and professional qualification.
- Scope, findings, unresolved discrepancies, and limitations.
- Evidence reference and audit history.
- Expiry/recheck rule where facts may change.

## Public status labels
Use precise labels such as “Listing reviewed”, “Documents received”, or “Record search completed on [date]” only when supported. Use “Title verified” only with a documented professional basis, defined scope, date, and clear limitations approved by counsel.

## Prohibited claims
- Guaranteed ownership or dispute-free property.
- Government approval inferred from an uploaded document.
- Legal title inferred from mutation, tax receipts, possession, or a single record alone.
- “Verified” badges without a written definition and evidence trail.

## Physical inspection
Reports must distinguish observed facts from client statements, include date/time and media provenance, and disclose areas not accessed or independently confirmed.
