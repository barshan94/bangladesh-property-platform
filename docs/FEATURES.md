# Feature Catalogue and Scope

## MVP candidates
### Public corporate experience
- Homepage, About, Why Choose Us, Contact.
- Service index and service detail pages.
- Expert consultation/contact CTA.
- Articles, FAQs, and video library.
- Testimonials with documented permission.

### Property marketplace
- Browse and search approved listings.
- Filters and property detail pages.
- Inquiry and site-visit request.
- Seller submission with moderation queue.
- Public/private media separation.

### Operations
- Staff login and role-based admin access.
- Listing review and publication controls.
- Lead categorization and assignment.
- Secure legal evaluation intake.
- Audit trail for sensitive actions.

## Later candidates
- Client dashboard and saved properties.
- Case status timeline and secure document exchange.
- Remote property inspection scheduling and reports.
- Power-of-attorney coordination, after legal review.
- Tenant and property management coordination.
- Multilingual content and time-zone-aware consultations.

## Excluded until separately approved
- Escrow, custody of client funds, automated legal determinations, guaranteed title certification, open user-to-user messaging, general legal practice outside property, and complex rent/accounting automation.

## Status
All items above are scope candidates, not implemented features. See ROADMAP.md for evidence-based status.
