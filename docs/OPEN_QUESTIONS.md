# Open Questions and Assumptions

Resolve these before committing to implementation choices.

## Company and operations
- What is the company/brand name and legal operating entity?
- Is the platform operated by a real estate agency, legal practice, service coordinator, or a partnership?
- Who is accountable for legal advice, inspections, listing review, and client support?
- Which physical office locations and contact channels can be publicly represented?

## Market and eligibility
- Is Bangladesh the only launch market?
- Which districts/cities are in scope at launch?
- Which overseas client groups are served?
- What eligibility checks apply to foreign nationals, dual nationals, NRBs, and heirs?
- Who provides final legal eligibility decisions?

## Marketplace
- Are listings supplied by the company, property owners, partner agents, or all three?
- What authority-to-list evidence is required?
- Is the marketplace public from launch or initially a curated catalogue?
- What does each listing status mean?
- Are brokerage, commission, or advertising fees planned?

## Services
- Which services can actually be delivered at launch?
- Which are legal representation, legal advice, administrative coordination, or third-party referrals?
- Which qualified professionals are engaged, and how are conflicts handled?
- What are service fees, consultation fees, refund terms, and response targets?
- Will remote property management include rent collection or custody of funds? If yes, obtain specialist legal and operational review.

## Product and technology
- Required launch languages: Bangla, English, or both?
- Preferred budget and acceptable recurring costs?
- Technology stack and hosting provider?
- Database, authentication, private storage, maps, email, messaging, analytics?
- Need client accounts at MVP, or can initial requests be handled through secure staff workflows?
- What are document size limits, retention periods, and deletion procedures?

## Governance
- Who approves legal content and public marketing claims?
- Who owns security incidents and data requests?
- What is the pilot acceptance criteria and launch date?
- What evidence is required to mark each roadmap milestone complete?
