# Project Charter

## Vision
Make it easier and safer for people in Bangladesh and overseas to discover property opportunities and obtain coordinated property-related assistance in Bangladesh.

## Product identity
A combined **real estate marketplace + property legal, verification, and remote-assistance service**. The corporate website is a core part of the product, not a decorative wrapper around listings.

## Problem
People living away from a property may struggle to inspect it, understand its records, coordinate local processes, manage tenants, or find accountable local assistance. Buyers and sellers also need clear property information and a trustworthy inquiry process.

## Product outcomes
- Help users discover and inquire about property listings.
- Help owners and eligible clients request clearly scoped property services.
- Support remote communication, document submission, and case-status visibility.
- Establish trust through transparent processes, expert information, educational content, and clear limitations.

## Non-goals for the initial release
- A general-purpose legal-services directory covering unrelated legal areas.
- A public document repository.
- Automated legal opinions or guaranteed title certification.
- Unrestricted foreign property acquisition facilitation.
- Unmoderated, automatically published listings.
- A full property-management accounting system unless separately scoped.

## Geography and legal scope
Initial focus: Bangladesh property matters. Every legal service, eligibility rule, workflow, and marketing claim requires current review by qualified Bangladesh counsel. Foreign-client eligibility must be assessed case by case.

## Business model
Not decided. Do not implement fees, commissions, subscriptions, escrow, or payment collection until pricing, legal, tax, and operational responsibilities are documented.

## Product principles
1. Trust before conversion.
2. Clear evidence and transparent limitations.
3. Privacy by default.
4. Human review for high-impact legal and verification decisions.
5. Mobile-first, accessible, bilingual-ready experience.
6. No feature marked complete without testable evidence.
