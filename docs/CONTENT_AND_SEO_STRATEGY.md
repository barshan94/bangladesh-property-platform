# Content and SEO Strategy

## Trust-led content architecture
- Home: concise company positioning, property discovery, service overview, trust evidence, expert CTA.
- About: operating entity, team, qualifications, geography, and service model.
- Why Choose Us: evidence-backed process, confidentiality, transparency, and limitations.
- Services: property-specific categories and individual service pages.
- Marketplace: descriptive, indexable listing pages with controlled publication.
- Knowledge hub: educational articles and practical Bangladesh property guides.
- Video library: expert explainers and process walkthroughs.
- FAQs: grouped by service and user journey.
- Testimonials: genuine, permissioned, attributable only as consent allows.
- Contact: phone, email, WhatsApp if supported, office details, and secure inquiry form.

## Editorial rules
- Legal content must be reviewed by qualified Bangladesh counsel and display review date and scope.
- Separate general information from legal advice.
- Avoid guarantees, fear-based marketing, misleading badges, and unsupported success rates.
- Do not publish confidential case details without documented consent and anonymization review.
- Identify authors/reviewers and correct outdated content promptly.

## SEO
- Human-readable URLs and unique page titles/descriptions.
- Canonical URLs, XML sitemap, robots policy, structured data only where accurate.
- Optimized WebP/modern image formats, responsive media, lazy loading below the fold.
- Internal links between services, FAQs, articles, and relevant listings.
- Avoid thin duplicate location pages and fake office/location claims.
- Measure search performance without collecting unnecessary personal data.

## Languages
English and Bangla are candidates. Confirm launch languages, translation review ownership, and whether legal translations require professional review.
