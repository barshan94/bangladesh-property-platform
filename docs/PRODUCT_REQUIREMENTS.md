# Product Requirements

## Product summary
A responsive corporate website and service platform combining a Bangladesh property marketplace with property legal, verification, and remote-assistance request workflows.

## Audiences and needs
- Buyers: find relevant listings, understand key facts, request information, and schedule visits.
- Sellers: submit property details and evidence, understand moderation, and receive inquiry updates.
- Owners abroad: request local inspections, document coordination, and property support.
- Heirs: understand and request assistance with succession, partition, mutation, and related matters.
- Tenants/landlords: find information and request help with property-related disputes.
- Staff: review listings, triage leads, manage cases, and communicate with clients.
- Experts/counsel: review assigned matters within a defined professional and access-control framework.

## Core requirements
### Corporate website
- Homepage with clear positioning, company introduction, property discovery entry, service highlights, credibility evidence, and calls to action.
- About, Why Choose Us, Contact, consultation, and expert-contact pages/sections.
- Service catalogue and individual service explanations.
- Knowledge hub with articles and property guides.
- Video library, FAQs, testimonials, and consent-based video testimonials.
- Mobile-responsive contact and call/WhatsApp actions where supported.

### Marketplace
- Search and filters by location, property type, price, area, bedrooms/bathrooms, and clearly defined verification status.
- Property detail page with media, location, dimensions, amenities, ownership/tenure information, and listing disclosures.
- Map pins and nearby landmarks, subject to accurate geocoding and privacy review.
- Inquiry, site-visit request, and seller submission.
- Human moderation before publication.
- Favorites and client tracking may be phased after MVP.

### Services and case intake
- Select service category and describe the issue.
- Secure private PDF/JPG upload with explicit file limits and consent.
- Confirmation and reference number.
- Staff triage, assignment, status history, and controlled client communication.
- No legal advice or outcome guarantee from automated forms or status labels.

## Quality attributes
- Mobile-first, accessible, responsive.
- Fast pages, optimized media, semantic HTML, search-friendly URLs.
- Strong authentication, authorization, privacy, auditability, and resilience.
- Clear error states and support contact.
- Maintainable code and automated tests.

## Acceptance principles
- Every feature has testable acceptance criteria.
- Sensitive data is never exposed to unauthorized roles.
- Public claims match actual operational capability.
- Legal workflows are approved by qualified local counsel.
