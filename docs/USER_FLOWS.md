# User Flows

## Visitor discovers a property
1. Open marketplace.
2. Search/filter by location, property type, and budget.
3. Review listing facts, media, disclosures, and status definitions.
4. Submit an inquiry or site-visit request.
5. Receive a confirmation and a clear next-step expectation.

## Seller submits a listing
1. Create an account or complete approved identity/contact verification.
2. Enter property details and authority-to-list information.
3. Upload permitted evidence to private storage.
4. Submit for human moderation.
5. Respond to clarification requests.
6. Listing reviewer approves, rejects, or requests changes with a recorded reason.
7. Only approved listing content becomes public.

## Overseas owner requests assistance
1. Select a service (for example inspection, title-record review, mutation assistance, or tenant coordination).
2. Complete eligibility and scope questions.
3. Submit contact details and a concise description.
4. Upload confidential documents through a secure channel.
5. Receive acknowledgment and case reference.
6. Staff triage, confirm scope/fees/required documents, and assign an appropriate professional.
7. Client receives progress updates and a final deliverable or closure explanation.

## Confidential legal evaluation
1. Select relevant property issue/service.
2. Read scope, privacy notice, and no-guarantee disclaimer.
3. Describe issue and provide contact details.
4. Upload documents privately.
5. Receive confirmation; submission does not itself create a lawyer-client relationship unless expressly agreed.
6. Authorized staff review and contact the client.

## Admin triage
1. Review incoming lead or case.
2. Check duplicates, urgency, completeness, and jurisdiction/eligibility.
3. Assign to authorized staff or qualified counsel.
4. Record status and actions.
5. Close with documented outcome and retention handling.
